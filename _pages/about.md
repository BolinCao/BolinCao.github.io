# Bolin Cao

I received my PhD in Psychology from **Trinity College Dublin**. My research uses neuroimaging and brain-network analysis to investigate three areas: dementia risk and prevention in midlife, brain reorganisation in patients with disorders of consciousness, and training-related brain plasticity in elite athletes.

## Research

### Dementia risk and prevention

My doctoral research used the **PREVENT Dementia** cohort to study cognitively healthy adults in midlife, before the onset of clinical symptoms. I examined how apolipoprotein E (APOE) ε4, cardiovascular and other modifiable risk factors, and cognitive-reserve-related experiences are associated with cognition and the functional architecture of the brain. This work combined directed functional connectivity, dynamic and higher-order brain interactions, machine learning, and multivariate modelling to identify early neural patterns associated with dementia risk and resilience.

### Disorders of consciousness

I study how severe brain injury alters the organisation and temporal dynamics of functional brain networks in patients with disorders of consciousness. This work aims to improve the characterisation of residual brain function and to inform more individualised approaches to clinical assessment and recovery.

### Athlete brain plasticity

I investigate how long-term intensive training shapes the human brain. Using resting-state functional magnetic resonance imaging in elite gymnasts and other trained populations, I examine network stability, dynamic reconfiguration, functional lateralisation, and inter-hemispheric collaboration, as well as the brain's response to short-term concussion.
