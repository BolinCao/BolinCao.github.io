# Academic Profile

My academic training is in psychology and cognitive neuroscience. I completed my master's degree in Psychology at **South China Normal University** and my PhD in Psychology at **Trinity College Dublin**.

My research focuses on **cognitive neuroscience and dementia prevention**, with particular interest in the neural mechanisms that shape cognitive health across midlife and ageing. I use neuroimaging, brain-network analysis, and multivariate modelling to examine early signatures of dementia risk and resilience before clinical symptoms emerge.

## Research focus

My current work asks how inherited risk, modifiable health factors, and cognitive-reserve-related experiences are reflected in the functional organisation of the brain during midlife. The broader aim is to identify neurobiological patterns that may help explain why some individuals remain cognitively resilient while others become more vulnerable to later-life cognitive decline.
