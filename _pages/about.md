<section class="home-section" id="research">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <div>
      <span class="section-eyebrow">RESEARCH</span>
      <h2>Three connected questions about brain organisation</h2>
    </div>
    <p>My work combines functional MRI, network neuroscience, multivariate statistics and machine learning to study how brain systems reorganise across clinical injury, disease risk and intensive experience.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-grid">
    <article class="research-card research-card--doc">
      <div class="research-visual research-visual--doc" aria-hidden="true"><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span></div>
      <span class="research-number">01</span>
      <h3>Disorders of consciousness</h3>
      <p>I study how severe brain injury alters the organisation, directionality and temporal dynamics of functional brain networks, with the aim of improving characterisation of residual brain function and recovery.</p>
      <div class="research-keywords">DoC · directed connectivity · dynamics</div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card research-card--dementia">
      <div class="research-visual research-visual--dementia" aria-hidden="true"><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span></div>
      <span class="research-number">02</span>
      <h3>Dementia risk & prevention</h3>
      <p>Using the PREVENT-Dementia programme, I investigate how inherited and modifiable risk factors relate to cognition and functional brain architecture during midlife, before clinical symptoms emerge.</p>
      <div class="research-keywords">APOE · midlife risk · cognitive resilience</div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card research-card--athlete">
      <div class="research-visual research-visual--athlete" aria-hidden="true"><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span></div>
      <span class="research-number">03</span>
      <h3>Athlete brain plasticity</h3>
      <p>I examine how long-term intensive training shapes network stability, dynamic reconfiguration, functional lateralisation and inter-hemispheric organisation in elite athletes.</p>
      <div class="research-keywords">training · plasticity · concussion</div>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
