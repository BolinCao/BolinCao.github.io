# Bolin Cao

I received my PhD in Psychology from **Trinity College Dublin**. My research focuses on cognitive neuroscience, brain-network organisation, cognitive ageing, and the prevention of dementia, with a particular interest in risk and protective factors during midlife. I also study disorders of consciousness and experience-dependent brain plasticity.

## Research

My main research programme investigates how inherited susceptibility, modifiable health factors, and cognitive-reserve-related experiences are reflected in the functional architecture of the brain before the onset of clinical symptoms. Using resting-state functional magnetic resonance imaging, directed and dynamic connectivity, higher-order interactions, and multivariate modelling, I study early neural signatures of dementia risk and resilience in cognitively healthy midlife adults.

My broader work examines functional reorganisation after severe brain injury and long-term neuroplasticity associated with intensive training.
